Turn ON while you fix failed images — this account is then used only for failed images and kept out of the automatic article generation.

Away mode

ON shares this Chrome's Google login with Flow2API so it can sign this account in to Google Labs again by itself while your laptop is closed. OFF deletes Flow2API's copy; the account then works only while this laptop is open.

Suno music

Turn ON if this Chrome is signed in to suno.com. Your Suno login is shared with Flow2API so it can make songs on your account. Turning this off stops syncing; it does not remove the account from Flow2API.
